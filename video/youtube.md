# YouTube upload

**Title:** Payday Room: two AI agents pay contributors, people approve (Flip the Switch demo)

**Description:**

Payday Room is a Telegram group wired up with Switch, where two Claude Code agents do the tedious part of paying contributors, and people keep every decision.

Tally reads what people say they did this month and applies the written payout rules. The lead fixes or approves the table in the thread. Treasurer pays the batch in USDC on Arc testnet. It sends only after two different approvers quote the batch id and a person presses Allow on Switch's approval card, and then it posts every receipt.

Both paydays in this video ran live on 6 November 2026. Testnet only: no real money moves.

Built for Flip the Switch (WeMakeDevs × Switch), Best Business Workflow track.
